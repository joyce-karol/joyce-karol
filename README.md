# Hi, I'm Joy
### Biomedical Scientist | Bioinformatician | Artist

I explore how transcriptomic data can reveal the molecular mechanisms behind tropical infectious diseases.

Currently, I am a PhD candidate at the Federal University of Bahia (UFBA), working at the intersection of:

- Transcriptomics
- Systems Biology
- Long non coning RNAs discovery

My research focuses on identifying molecular signatures associated with disease progression, host-pathogen interactions, and immune responses.

I also work in the educational initiatives to make science more accessible throughout Latin America.
